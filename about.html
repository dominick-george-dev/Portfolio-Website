About me

Building software, now specialising in securing it

I'm a Cardiff-based software engineer, originally from Talgarth in mid Wales. I graduated from Cardiff University in 2026 with a 2:1 in Applied Software Engineering, and I'm now studying for the NCSC-certified MSc in Cyber Security and Technology there, graduating in July 2027.

My path so far

I started programming at school through GCSE and A-level Computer Science. My A-level project, a Python and SQLite app for my mum's camping business, is what convinced me to become a software engineer.

My degree taught me to ship software with other people: Agile client projects such as an applicant-tracking system for NHS Wales, automated builds and tests in CI pipelines, and full-stack work in Java/Spring Boot, Python/Flask and React/Node.js. Alongside it I helped found Cardiff Autonomous Racing, where I led the path-planning team and built the ROS 2 module that plans the car's racing line.

The work I enjoyed most was the security side: hashing and salting passwords, adding two-factor authentication, preventing SQL injection. That's why I chose the MSc. I want to be the engineer who builds the feature and understands how it could be attacked.

What I'm looking for

Graduate or junior roles in application and product security or full-stack software engineering, ideally where the two overlap: building secure-by-design web applications and helping teams catch vulnerabilities before they ship. I'm looking for roles starting after I graduate in summer 2027, based in Cardiff and open to roles across the UK and remote.
